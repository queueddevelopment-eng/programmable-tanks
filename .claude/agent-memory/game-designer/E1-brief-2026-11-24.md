## Rules setup (v0.5 as built)
- Deck: `V05 = dict(V03_DECK); V05[ELSE] = 2`; pile = `shared_deck(V05) + SPECIALS` (45 cards, pickup module off). `cfg.deck` = V05 plus 1 per SPECIALS entry (experiments7 pattern).
- `Config(cooldown=1, build_behind=True, hunt_avoid=True, shot_clock=10, clock_roles=True, clock_leader=True, clock_cards=True, initiative=True, clock_first=(seed + r) % 2)` for round r (attacker rotates between rounds, RULES "Shot clock").
- Bench/spar: as experiments7 (`Random(77)`, deck V05 without specials).

## Match structure (chosen readings; alternatives in Questions)
- **Rail** = the rail as it stands at round end, pit-stop plays included. Spent specials reset (each round is a fresh `play()`).
- **Layout** fixed for the match (one layout card per match). Players keep seats.
- Between rounds, everything not in a rail or a hand (pit-stop discards, returned cards) is shuffled back: pile = full deck minus both rails' non-chassis cards (`Counter(rail) - CHASSIS`) minus both hands.
- **Winner:** keeps its rail and leftover hand, and plays round-2 pit stops normally (locked at programming only).
- **(a)** Loser returns rail (non-chassis) and hand, draws 5, rebuilds from chassis + 5.
- **(b)** Loser picks its rail up into its hand (pool = rail non-chassis + hand), draws 2, rebuilds.
- **(c)** Loser keeps any rail cards, returns its hand, draws 5. Keeping is weakly dominant, so the proxy keeps all: pool = rail non-chassis + 5 new.
- **Draw round:** both count as losers; each rebuilds per the option against the other's round-end rail.
- **Match:** first to 3, cap 9 rounds (report unfinished). Rounds 3+ repeat the rule.
- **Rebuild proxies:** expert loser = `X.build(rng, avail, cfg_r, grid, [L], spar, "expert")` (bench = [L]: both seats vs the locked rail). Novice loser = best of 6 random legal programs from avail with Fire and a mover, chosen by `X.value` vs L in its actual seat. Round played via `X.round_with_stops(..., X.STOPS)`.

## Counter (round 2 of every decided round 1)
- Four no-stop duels vs L on the match layout: seat {own, swapped} x clock_first {0, 1}.
- **Counter** = wins (not draws) the actual condition and >= 3 of 4 (X = 75%). The one allowed loss covers tie-break artefacts (initiative order, first attacker); the actual condition must be won because that is the round the loser plays.
- **Expert counter exists** = the expert rebuild meets it. This is an upper bound (full search, perfect knowledge of L).
- **Novice reach** = the novice pick meets it. **Density** = share of 200 random legal programs from avail that meet it (comparable to September's ~1%).
- **Undone** = the expert counter met the definition but lost round 2 (winner's pit stops or own draws).

## Metrics and pass lines (fixed by the plan; not moved)
- **P1:** the round-1 loser wins round 2 in 35-60%. Population: EvE+NvN with a decided round 1.
- **P2:** an expert counter exists in >= 80% of deals. Population: deals where L was expert-built.
- **P3:** the round-1 winner wins the match < 75%. Population: EvE+NvN, finished matches.
- **Also report:**
  - novice reach and density;
  - undone %;
  - round-2 draws;
  - rounds per match;
  - loser pool size;
  - stuck % of rounds 2+ (`stall_info`);
  - expert match share in EvN/NvE vs Z (flag a drop > 10 points: skill erased).
- **Verdict per option:** pass if P1-P3 all hold. Inconclusive if a 95% interval straddles a line; then rerun that letter only at N=80.

## Sample, noise, runtime
- N = 40 matches per board x 6 boards = 240 matches (80 EvE, 80 NvN, 40 EvN, 40 NvE).
- Noise: ~120 decided even rounds gives about +/-9 on P1/P3; ~130 expert-L deals gives about +/-7 on P2.
- Runtime estimate: ~4.5k plays for round 1, ~5k per debug letter, ~18k for Z. About 7 s per match on 2 cores, ~25 min for ZYABC and ~10 min for FN.

## Paper cost between rounds
The winner's rail stays on the table untouched (zero lookups) and specials flip back. Everyone: discards are shuffled in.
- A: the loser returns its cards, shuffles, draws 5.
- B: the loser picks its rail up and draws 2. No new decision.
- C: the loser chooses which rail cards to keep, returns the rest and its hand, shuffles, draws 5.
- F: the same as A, plus the winner skips its pit stops.
- N: the same as A, plus a new layout is set up.
