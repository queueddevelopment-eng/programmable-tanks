# E2 brief: stalls under card-language overtime (2026-11-24, pending)

**Variants**

| | clock_cards / clock_path | clock_attack | clock_hold | paper cost per tick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K comparison | False / True | - | - | route search: not paper (m) |
| V control, the rule | True / False | `IF_ENEMY FIRE ELSE HUNT` | `IF_ENEMY FIRE ELSE TRACK` | 1 condition, 1 remembered bit, Hunt |
| P turn when blocked | True | `IF_ENEMY FIRE IF_BLOCKED RIGHT HUNT` | as V | 2 conditions, no Otherwise, Hunt |
| Q moving holder | True | as V | `IF_ENEMY FIRE ELSE TRACK HUNT` | as V, holder also Hunts every other tick |
| R | True | as P | as Q | sum of P and Q |

Q is nearest to the rejected symmetric clock; it differs (different programs, holder half speed). It needs a clear margin over P to count.

**Per-layout score (E5 reuses this; one function, `layout_score`).** Per layout: N; stuck % (`stall_info`, clock+10 quiet ticks), split into apart, dance and parked; seat % (A wins / decided EvE+NvN) with its decided count; kills %; draws %; never hit %; overtime pit falls per round; autopilot share. **Keep** iff stuck ≤2% at N ≥300 and seat 45–55%. Shuffled is a generator, not a layout: report it as a stress row and don't judge it. The deck for E2 = the 5 presets.

**Mechanism output.** For each stuck round: seed, layout, attacker seat, and the cycle period of (pos, facing) for both tanks over the stretch. This answers why before any fix.

**Stages and N.** At 60/board, one stuck round is 1.7%. A layout with a true 4% passes 30% of the time, which is useless for a 2% line.
1. Smoke test at N=6.
2. Screen K V P Q R at 60/board: about 25 min. Drop any variant with ALL stuck >V.
3. Decide at 300/board on K, V and the best alternative: about 60–75 min. ≤6/300 passes; a true 4% passes 5% of the time, a true 2% 61%. Pooled seat ±3; per-layout seat ±7.5, so a layout outside 45–55 whose interval overlaps the band reruns at 600.
4. If curation drops a layout, confirm the kept deck on fresh seeds (`+50000`) at 300.

Background runs, one at a time. Output to `results/results8.txt`, `results8b.txt`, `results8c.txt`.

**Guard rails (written now).** Don't adopt an alternative if kills < V−10, autopilot share >28% (K+10), or expert skill share moves >10 from V. A stall "fixed" by overtime pit falls is reported as such.
