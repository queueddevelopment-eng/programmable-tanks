# Tick sheet: hand-run round

The board and tokens hold the state, and the sheet records **what happened**. Write only what changed. The sheet also does two jobs without tokens:
- **Cooldown:** a G in your tank's row on the previous tick means Fire and Breach fizzle this tick.
- **Stuck:** a P written on a tick means that tank skips the next 2 ticks.

## Tokens on each tank's mat (not on the sheet)

| Token | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pointer clip | sits on the rail card to read next (a second clip goes on the overtime card) |
| T/F disc | the last condition, for Otherwise (overtime cards have their own disc) |
| Hit token | the tank was hit (or drove into a pit) since its last If hit was read, even if a shield soaked all the damage |
| Shield chips (0–2) | each soaks 1 damage |
| Overcharge chip | the next hit is doubled |
| Detour token | from Hunt step 2a |
| Counter on a Repeat ×3 card | the ticks left on it |
| Wall chips (0–2) | the walls this tank has raised |
| Face-down card | a spent special |

On the table: the clock track (0–10), an overtime marker, and an "attacker" marker.

## Codes for the Events column

G gun went off · Z fizzled · H1 / H2 / H4 hit for that much (after the shield) · S shield used · O Overcharge armed · W wall raised · X wall breached · P drove into a pit · D detour placed · I idled · K kept / R restarted at a pit stop
