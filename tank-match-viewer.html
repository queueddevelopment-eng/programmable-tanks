Programmable Tanks

Build each tank's program from cards, then run the match. Once it starts, the tanks follow their cards on their own. In the draft rules you draw your cards, the board fights back, pit stops let you patch your program mid-round, rare specials and pickups add a twist, and a shot clock stops rounds from stalling.

Tap a square: empty, then wall, then pit, then empty again.

    What's new in the draft rules
    • Shot clock. If nobody is hit for 10 ticks, each tank's program is set aside and it runs a printed overtime program, starting from its first card. The tank ahead on armor must attack, so nobody can sit on a lead: it runs If in sights · Fire · Otherwise · Hunt. The other holds: If in sights · Fire · Otherwise · Face enemy. With armor level, the roles take turns. After the next hit, both tanks go back to their own programs where they left off.
    • v0.4: Otherwise. True when the last condition was false: If in sights · Fire · Otherwise · Hunt.
    • v0.4: specials (rare, shuffled into the shared deck): Shield soaks 1 damage from the next hit, Overcharge doubles your next hit, Spread shot fires all four ways even while the gun cools. Each works once, then the card is spent: any statement whose action is spent is skipped, so a loop in front of a special fires it once. It stays spent after a pit stop.
    • Pickups. A matching pair sits on the board from the start, visible while you program. Drive over one to take it: a shield charge, an overcharge, or a spread shot on your next Fire. "If pickup ahead" sees one in a straight line.
    • Pits. Driving into a pit costs 2 armor and the next 2 ticks. Shots pass over pits. "If blocked" sees a pit ahead, and Hunt steers around them.
    • Hunt never turns to face a wall or pit. If turning toward the enemy would face one, the tank drives on, or turns the other way if it is boxed in. A Turn left or Turn right before Hunt now sticks.
    • Raise wall puts a wall on the empty square behind you, two per tank per round. It covers your back and can never block your own path or gun. Breach fires a round that removes the first wall in line. It uses the gun, so it shares the cooldown with Fire, and it cannot hurt a tank.
    • Dealt hands. Fire ×2, Hunt and Forward are your chassis and always available. Everything else comes from one shared deck. You start with 5 cards in hand.
    • Pit stops after ticks 10 and 20. Each tank draws a card, then may play one card from its hand into the program, adding it or replacing a card. The program then restarts from its first card.
